Short Description

In line with the mission and vision of the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS), the KLEF Student Branch Chapter brings together students, researchers and faculty who share an interest in intelligent transportation systems.

The chapter aims to build a collaborative community at Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation, connect students with the global ITSS research network, and give them hands-on experience with the technologies behind safer and smarter mobility.

Chapter Activities

The chapter organises expert talks, Distinguished Lecturer visits, hands-on workshops, student projects and outreach in four focus areas.

  • Automotive radarFMCW and 4D imaging radar for measuring range and speed in all weather.
  • Sensor fusionCombining radar, camera and LiDAR for autonomous-vehicle perception.
  • Driver assistanceAdaptive cruise control, emergency braking and vehicle control.
  • Connected mobilityV2X communication, smart traffic management and road safety.
