About us

Who we are

We are a student chapter of the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS), the IEEE community that applies electrical engineering and information technology to transportation.

Our members explore how vehicles sense the road, how traffic systems communicate, and how data and AI can reduce road accidents and congestion. We organise expert talks, hands-on workshops and student projects, and connect KLEF students with the global ITSS research community.

What we work on

Focus areas

Sensing

Automotive radar

FMCW and 4D imaging radar, and how vehicles measure range and speed in rain, fog and darkness.

Perception

Sensor fusion

Combining radar, camera and LiDAR so autonomous vehicles understand the road around them.

Control

Driver assistance

Adaptive cruise control, emergency braking and the control systems behind safer driving.

Systems

Connected mobility

V2X communication, smart traffic management and AI for safer Indian roads.

Membership

Join IEEE ITSS

Any KLEF student can join. As an ITSS member you get access to the Society's journals, conferences, Distinguished Lecturer talks and a global network of transportation researchers.

  1. Become an IEEE Student Member at ieee.org/membership/join.
  2. Add the Intelligent Transportation Systems Society to your membership.
  3. Contact the chapter to take part in our events and projects.
