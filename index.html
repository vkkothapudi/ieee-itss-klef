Short Description

In line with the mission of the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS), the Student Branch Chapter at Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation (KLEF) brings together students and faculty who share an interest in intelligent transportation.

The chapter organises expert talks, hands-on workshops and student projects on automotive radar, sensor fusion, driver assistance, connected vehicles and AI for safer roads, and connects KLEF students with the global ITSS research community.
